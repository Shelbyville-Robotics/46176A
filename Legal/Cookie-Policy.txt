# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

**Entity:** Greeks LLC

Greeks LLC (“Greeks LLC,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, stored, and used when you visit websites operated by Greeks LLC, including websites associated with **Bacon, Egg & Cheese™** and **HappyMeal** (collectively, the “Websites”).

The Websites are primarily intended to provide information about robotics teams, projects, activities, competitions, and related work.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Websites are designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and understand Website traffic.

Information may be collected through several components of our Website infrastructure, including GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, domain and DNS services, and our own backup and monitoring systems.

### 1.1 Information Automatically Generated When You Visit

When you visit a Website, certain technical information may be automatically processed by the services used to host, protect, and operate the Websites.

This information may include:

* IP address;
* Date and time of the request;
* Requested webpage or resource;
* Referring page or website;
* Browser type;
* Operating system;
* Device or connection information;
* General geographic information that may be derived from an IP address;
* Technical information concerning the connection; and
* Security or diagnostic information.

This information may be used for purposes such as website security, preventing abuse, diagnosing technical problems, maintaining availability, and understanding general Website traffic.

GitHub states that visitors to GitHub Pages sites have their IP addresses logged and stored for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed into GitHub.

## 2. GitHub Pages

The Websites may be hosted using **GitHub Pages** through the Greeks LLC organization.

GitHub Pages provides the infrastructure used to publish the Websites.

Because GitHub operates the hosting infrastructure, GitHub may independently process certain technical information associated with visits to the Websites.

GitHub's own privacy practices apply to information that GitHub processes through its services.

For additional information regarding GitHub's handling of information, visitors should review GitHub's applicable privacy documentation.

Greeks LLC does not control all information processed by GitHub as part of providing GitHub Pages.

## 3. Cloudflare

The Websites use **Cloudflare** services to assist with network security, traffic management, connectivity, and related Website functionality.

Cloudflare may process technical information associated with visitors' requests, including information necessary to route, secure, and protect Website traffic.

Cloudflare may also use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies in providing its services. Cloudflare states that such cookies can be used for functions including traffic management and protection against malicious traffic.

The Websites do not use Cloudflare's infrastructure to intentionally identify individual visitors beyond information reasonably necessary for Website operation, security, and traffic analysis.

Cloudflare's own privacy policies and terms govern information processed by Cloudflare as a service provider.

## 4. Website Traffic Information

Greeks LLC may collect basic Website traffic information to understand how the Websites are being used and to identify technical problems.

This information may include aggregate or technical measurements such as:

* Number of visits;
* Number of page requests;
* Frequently accessed pages;
* Approximate traffic patterns;
* Date and time of visits;
* Referring sources;
* Browser or device categories;
* Basic performance information; and
* Other non-content traffic measurements.

Where possible, traffic information is used in an aggregated or summarized form rather than to identify individual visitors.

## 5. Backup Traffic Data

Greeks LLC maintains its own backup and monitoring systems, which may be hosted on privately controlled computing equipment.

These systems may retain limited Website traffic or technical information for purposes including:

* Backup;
* Redundancy;
* Security;
* Troubleshooting;
* Availability monitoring;
* Technical diagnostics; and
* Comparing traffic measurements with third-party infrastructure.

Greeks LLC does not intentionally use these backup systems to create individual visitor profiles.

Where practical, traffic information retained by these systems is limited to the minimum information reasonably necessary for the stated purposes.

## 6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Websites may use cookies or similar technologies as part of the infrastructure required to provide, secure, and operate the Websites.

These may include technologies used by Cloudflare or other infrastructure providers.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as:

* Security;
* Traffic management;
* Preventing malicious or abusive requests;
* Maintaining Website functionality;
* Technical diagnostics; and
* Measuring Website performance or traffic.

The Websites do not intentionally use cookies for behavioral advertising.

You may be able to control or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect Website functionality.

## 7. Information You Voluntarily Provide

If a Website provides a contact form, email link, application, submission form, or similar feature, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

* Name;
* Email address;
* Message content;
* Team or organization information; or
* Other information included in your submission.

Greeks LLC uses information voluntarily submitted through these features for the purpose for which it was provided, such as responding to a message, processing a request, or communicating with a user.

Greeks LLC does not require visitors to provide personal information merely to view publicly available Website content.

## 8. How We Use Information

Information collected through the Websites may be used to:

* Operate the Websites;
* Host and deliver Website content;
* Protect the Websites from malicious traffic;
* Maintain Website security;
* Diagnose technical problems;
* Monitor availability;
* Understand general traffic patterns;
* Improve Website performance and design;
* Respond to communications;
* Maintain backups;
* Investigate abuse or unauthorized activity;
* Comply with applicable legal obligations; and
* Protect the rights, property, and security of Greeks LLC and others.

We do not intentionally use Website traffic information to create advertising profiles of visitors.

## 9. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Unless specifically stated on a particular Website, Greeks LLC does not intentionally request or collect sensitive personal information merely for the purpose of allowing someone to browse the Websites.

The Websites are not designed to require visitors to provide:

* Social Security numbers;
* Financial account information;
* Credit card information;
* Government identification numbers;
* Passwords for unrelated services; or
* Other highly sensitive information.

Visitors should not submit sensitive personal information through general contact forms or other Website features unless specifically requested and appropriate.

Greeks LLC does not sell personal information collected through the Websites as a source of advertising or data-broker revenue.
