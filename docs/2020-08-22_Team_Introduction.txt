VEX V5 Robotics Team Archive
Team: Robotnik

Team introduction:
Robotnik is a VEX V5 robotics team focused on learning engineering, programming, CAD, documentation, and teamwork. This archive contains development notes and planning material from the season.

Goals:
- Build a reliable competition robot.
- Learn the VEX V5 ecosystem.
