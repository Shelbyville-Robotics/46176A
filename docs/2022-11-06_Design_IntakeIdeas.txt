Intake Design Ideas
Team: Bumblebee

Concept A: Simple roller intake
Pros:
- Few parts
- Easy to repair
- Simple control logic

Cons:
- May have difficulty with inconsistent game objects

Concept B: Multi-roller intake
Pros:
- More contact points
- Potentially smoother intake

Cons:
- More weight
- More motors or gearing may be required

Testing plan:
Build quick prototypes before committing to a final mechanism.
