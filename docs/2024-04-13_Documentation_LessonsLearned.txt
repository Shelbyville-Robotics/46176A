Lessons Learned
Team: Goblin machine
Season: 2023-2024

1. Prototype mechanisms before building the final version.
2. Keep electronics accessible for troubleshooting.
3. Test autonomous routines repeatedly instead of judging them from a single successful run.
4. Record why a design was changed, not just what changed.
5. A simple mechanism that works consistently can be more useful than a complicated mechanism that frequently fails.
