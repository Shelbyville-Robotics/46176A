Reliability Review
Team: Robotnik

Review each subsystem using these questions:

Drivetrain:
- Does it drive straight?
- Are wheels and shafts secure?
- Can a wheel be replaced quickly?

Intake:
- Does it jam?
- Can the rollers be accessed?
- Are gears protected?

Scoring mechanism:
- Does it repeat the same motion?
- Are hard stops or sensors needed?
- Can damaged parts be replaced quickly?

Electronics:
- Are cables labeled?
- Is the brain easy to access?
- Are batteries easy to swap?
