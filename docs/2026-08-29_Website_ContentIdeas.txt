Robotics Website Content Ideas

Possible public pages:
- Team history
- Current robot
- Previous robots
- Engineering notebook excerpts
- Programming resources
- CAD gallery
- Competition schedule
- Competition results
- Team members and roles
- Outreach projects
- Awards

Archive idea:
Use dates in filenames so the website can sort engineering records chronologically.

Suggested filename format:
YYYY-MM-DD_Category_FileName.ext

Examples:
2026-08-29_Design_DrivetrainRevision.txt
2026-09-03_Code_AutonomousRoutine.cpp
2026-09-10_Competition_EventReport.txt
